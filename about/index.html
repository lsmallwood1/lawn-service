How It Started

In 2016, founder Marcus Deland was mowing lawns around Seminole Heights on weekends to save up for a truck. Word of mouth turned a side hustle into a second crew, then a third. A decade later, Green Knack runs a dozen-person team across the Tampa Bay area — but the standard hasn't changed: show up when we say we will, do the work right, and answer the phone when a customer calls.

What We're Not

We're not a national franchise dispatching whoever's available that day, and we're not a guy with a truck who might not show up during busy season. We're a local company with dedicated routes, named crew leads, and a schedule built to hold.

How We Treat The Land

Tampa Bay's grass, soil, and storm season are specific to this part of Florida — and generic, one-size-fits-all lawn care doesn't work here. Every plan we build accounts for grass type, seasonal growth patterns, and the realities of a Gulf Coast climate.

Involved In The Neighborhoods We Serve

Every spring, Green Knack runs a free mow-a-thon for elderly and disabled homeowners in the neighborhoods we service — no strings attached. We also sponsor a Little League team in Seminole Heights and donate cleanup services to a handful of local nonprofits each year.

It's a small piece of the business, but it's the part the team is proudest of.
