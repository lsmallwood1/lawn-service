Shrub & Hedge Care

Shaping That Keeps Plants Healthy, Not Just Neat

Overgrown hedges block windows, hide house numbers, and eventually hollow out from the inside. We shape on a schedule that keeps yours full, healthy, and sharp-edged year-round.

What’s Included

What's Included In Every Visit

  • Hand and power shearing of hedges to a clean, level line
  • Selective pruning of ornamental shrubs to maintain natural shape
  • Debris blown clear and hauled away, not left in the bed
  • Health check for pests, fungus, or dieback with a heads-up if we spot something
  • Timing built around Florida's growing season, not a fixed calendar date

Timed To Florida’s Growing Season

Tampa Bay shrubs put on most of their growth from spring through the height of summer. Prune too early and you’ll cut off the season’s flush; prune too late and you’re fighting overgrowth into fall. We schedule shaping visits around how your specific plants are actually growing, not a generic quarterly date.

Pre-Storm Trimming

Ahead of hurricane season, we offer a pre-storm trim focused on removing top-heavy growth and weak branches near windows, roofs, and power lines — a small step that can meaningfully reduce storm damage risk.

Common Tampa Bay Plantings We Work With

Viburnum, ligustrum, podocarpus, ixora, hibiscus, and boxwood hedges are the most common shrubs we shape across the neighborhoods we serve, alongside crape myrtles and azaleas that need a lighter, more selective hand.
