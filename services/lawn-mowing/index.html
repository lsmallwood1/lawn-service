Built Around Florida Grass, Not A Generic Schedule

Most of Tampa Bay is planted in St. Augustine, with pockets of Zoysia and Bahia depending on the neighborhood and how much shade a yard gets. Each grows differently, and each punishes the wrong mowing height — cut St. Augustine too short in July and you’ll be fighting stress and weeds by August. We set your mowing height and frequency based on what’s actually growing in your yard, then adjust with the seasons.

Weekly Or Biweekly — Your Call

Tampa Bay’s wet season (roughly June through September) pushes most lawns to weekly mowing to keep up with growth. In the drier months, many customers step down to biweekly without any drop in appearance. We’ll recommend a frequency at quote time and you can adjust it any month.

Striping On Request

Want that crosshatched, freshly-cut look you see on ballfields? Ask your crew lead — striping is available at no extra charge on lawns mowed weekly.
