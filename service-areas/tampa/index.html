St. Augustine Grass & Tampa's Wet Season

St. Augustine is the dominant lawn grass across Tampa, prized for its thick, shade-tolerant growth — but it's also sensitive to being cut too short, which is a common mistake among lawn services that don't adjust for grass type. We mow Tampa lawns at 3.5–4 inches to keep St. Augustine dense and resistant to weeds.

Tampa's wet season, roughly June through September, can push growth fast enough that biweekly mowing leaves a lawn looking shaggy within days. Most of our Tampa customers move to weekly service for those months and step back down once growth slows in the fall.

Hurricane Season Readiness

We offer pre-storm hedge and tree-adjacent trimming ahead of hurricane season, and prioritize existing Tampa customers for debris cleanup after any named storm affects the area.
